Privacy Policy

Transparency and trust in every hand-crafted interaction.

1. Introduction

At HunarHub, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, list a product, or communicate with other users. This includes:

  • Account details (Name, Email, Phone)
  • Business details for entrepreneurs
  • Transaction history and payment info
  • Communications within the platform

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to provide and improve our services, process transactions, and maintain a secure community. We use data to:

  • Verify entrepreneur accounts
  • Facilitate orders and service requests
  • Send important platform updates
  • Prevent fraudulent activities

4. Data Protection

We implement robust security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure. Your sensitive payment information is encrypted and handled by secure third-party processors.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through your dashboard settings. For any specific data requests, you can contact our support team.
