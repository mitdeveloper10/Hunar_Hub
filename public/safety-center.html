Safety Center

Creating a secure environment for local commerce.

For Buyers

  • Look for the "Verified" badge on entrepreneur profiles to ensure authenticity.
  • Read community reviews before placing high-value custom service requests.
  • Keep all communications within the HunarHub messaging system.

For Entrepreneurs

  • Never share your account passwords or OTPs with anyone claiming to be staff.
  • Only deliver goods/services once the order is officially confirmed in your dashboard.
  • Report suspicious customer behavior immediately to our admin team.
